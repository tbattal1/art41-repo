**Governance pattern:** Federal SOE (CODESP) landlord + multiple private operators. **Note: the 2019 situation (before the 2022 privatization / APS transformation).**

---

## Structural summary
- **Landlord:** CODESP (Companhia Docas do Estado de São Paulo) — federal sociedade de economia mista (União 99.97 %; constituted 1980); landlord model under Lei 8.630/93 (operations transferred to private terminals, completed 1999). Under SEP-PR + ANTAQ as regulators. "Santos Port Authority" adopted as a trade name in October 2019; change of the razão social in 2020/2022 (post-window).
- **Operators:** Santos Brasil (Tecon Santos — the largest container terminal in Brazil per company filings, listed), BTP (APM + MSC), DP World Santos (formerly Embraport), Libra. Multiple independent private operators.
