# ART-41 — Case coding sheet: VALENCIA (#5)
**Coding date:** 2026-07-08 (Batch 2) | **Codebook:** v2.2 at coding; reconciled under v2.6 (K1–K16) on 2026-11-03 | **Status:** locked | **Window:** governance as of end-2019
**Governance pattern:** State port authority within the national Puertos del Estado system; plural private container operators; coordinated and socially embedded.

## Structural summary
- Autoridad Portuaria de Valencia (APV) — public body within the national Puertos del Estado system; governed by the Ley de Puertos (Real Decreto Legislativo 2/2011, TRLPEMM), Art. 26.
- APV acts as landlord and grants concessions to terminal operators (TiL/MSC, Noatum).
- Container operators: MSC Terminal Valencia (TiL) + Noatum Container Terminal (APM/Noatum) — ≥2 independent operators, ~100 % private operation.
- Chair appointed centrally (ministerial order published in the BOE); national oversight by Puertos del Estado.

## C1 — Regulatory autonomy → **0.30**
- **Subject rule (K9):** APV is the landlord and grants concessions to TiL/MSC → subject = APV.
- **Status (2019):** Public body within the national Puertos del Estado system; Ley de Puertos (RD Leg 2/2011) Art. 26.
- **Control:** Operational autonomy exists (Art. 26.k, tariff approval; Art. 26.l, concessions) BUT national oversight by Puertos del Estado + central appointment of the chair (ministerial order, BOE).
- **Score: 0.30** ("legal entity exists, operational decisions within the national system"). Below Gdańsk (0.50, its own S.A.) because APV is integrated into a unitary national system.
- Coder's note: the alternative of 0.50 (Gdańsk analogy) was considered in the working file and not adopted; 0.30 confirmed as final (author decision, 2026-07-08; analysed matrix).
