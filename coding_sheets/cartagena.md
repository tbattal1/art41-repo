**Governance pattern:** Colombian landlord model — state grantor + private sociedades portuarias (concessionaire-operators).

---

## Structural summary
- **Model:** Ley 1ª/1991 (Estatuto de Puertos Marítimos) → landlord; the concessionaire receives only an operating right, ownership remains with the State. Superintendencia (today Supertransporte) + ANI as grantor-regulator.
- **Operator:** SPRC (Sociedad Portuaria Regional de Cartagena, concession 007/1993, extended from 20 to 40 years → 2033) + Contecar (003/2008) = **Grupo Puerto de Cartagena** (private, container-dominant). Also in the bay: Compas/Central Cartagena (bulk/general, small).

---

## C1 — Regulatory autonomy → **0.30**
- **Subject:** the state grantor (Superintendencia/ANI). SPRC/Contecar are concessionaire-operators (their freedom is written to C2).
- **Evidence:** The grantor is a national-government structure: concession modifications/extensions are government-approved (court record: Ministerio de Transporte, Consejo Asesor; a major expansion requires a Decreto) → legal structure exists but decisions sit within the national system → 0.30.
- **Sources:** Consejo de Estado case (Superintendencia competence, government approval); revistas.upr (Ley 1ª/1991 landlord).

## C2 — Ownership hybridity → **0.70** (case-by-case calibration, author decision)
- Container >80 % private BUT a single dominant group (SPRC + Contecar = Grupo Puerto de Cartagena; separate concessions, same economic group). The other sociedades in the bay are non-container. → ">80 % private, single dominant operator" → 0.70 (Mersin precedent).
- **Sources:** puertocartagena.com (Grupo); Consejo de Estado, Sala de Consulta y Servicio Civil, Concepto 11001-03-06-000-2018-00213-00 (SPRC constituted by Escritura Pública No. 3427 de 1993, Notaría Tercera de Cartagena, as a sociedad anónima under Ley 1ª/1991 — "sociedades anónimas, constituidas con capital privado, público, o mixto"; the Nación holds 36,700 shares = 1.82 %, the remainder being private capital; https://www.consejodeestado.gov.co/documentos/boletines/222/11001-03-06-000-2018-00213-00.pdf, accessed 2026-11-03) [previously: D&B (SPRC private)].
- **Source check 2026-11-03:** the Consejo de Estado concept 2018-00213 states that the Nación holds 1.82 % of SPRC's shares, and press reports (El Universal; El Heraldo, 21.12.2016) put the Distrito de Cartagena's stake at ~2.1–2.3 %; i.e., SPRC is a predominantly (≈96 %) privately held sociedad anónima with minority public shareholders rather than a 100 % private company, which differs in degree from the coded shorthand "SPRC private"; the >80 % private threshold used for C2 is unaffected; flagged for author review.
