## Structural summary
- **Landlord + regulator:** Busan Port Authority (BPA) — established 16 January 2004 under the Port Authority Act (2003); a public corporation under MOF, capitalized by the in-kind contribution of the Port of Busan. Market-type public corporation (until 2023).
- **Operators (≥2 independent private/JV):** DP World (66 % of PNC), PSA, Hutchison, HMM, Macquarie Korea Infrastructure Fund, CJ Logistics, Hanjin (HJNC), PNIT, BNCT, and others. "Five-plus separate terminal operating companies."
- **Financial structure:** **Fixed rent by berth area** (renewable, actively restructured) — NOT a fixed long concession.

- **(b) Financial lock-in = 0.33 [K15].** Branch A-like but a **renewable, area-based lease system** (no fixed long concession): fixed rent by berth area, active restructuring (volume-linked working group), competitive re-tendering.   - Documented uncertainty: the exact lease tenor could not be sourced; under K15 no estimated tenor figure enters the truth table.
- **(c) Social legitimacy gap = 0.** No operationally effective port-worker strike found in 2015–2019; trucker strikes in 2022 (outside the window); Hanjin bankruptcy 2016 = financial collapse, not a dispute.
- **Mean:** mean(0.33, 0.33, 0) = **0.22**.
- **Profile note:** Low-lock-in, dynamic, flexible-lease landlord port. The exact opposite of Port Klang (0.56, locked by fixed long concessions) — the "flexible lease vs fixed concession" axis.
