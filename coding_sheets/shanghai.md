## Structural summary
- **Operator:** SIPG (Shanghai International Port (Group) Co., Ltd.) — **sole (exclusive) operator** of all public terminals of the Port of Shanghai; majority state-owned.
- **Chronology:** January 2003 restructuring from the Shanghai Port Administration → June 2005 joint-stock company → 26 October 2006 listing on the Shanghai Stock Exchange (China's first listed port operator, 600018.SH).
- **Ownership:** Shanghai SASAC ~44 % ultimate control; state-linked total >70 % (31 Dec 2019, SIPG Annual Report: Shanghai SASAC 31.36 % direct + 12.37 % via four SASAC entities = 43.73 %, actual controller; China Merchants-linked Ya Ji 25.15 % + CMPD 1.62 % = 26.77 %; COSCO Shipping 15.00 %; state-linked ≈ 85 %).
- **Regulator:** Shanghai Municipal Transportation Commission / port administration (administrative function separate from SIPG).

## C1 — Regulatory autonomy → **0.30**
- **Subject rule (K9):** Municipal port administration / the state. SIPG is the exclusive operator and does not lease to third parties → single-operator branch → subject = public side.
- **Evidence:** SIPG is corporatized and listed (legal personality + operational commercial autonomy) BUT majority state-owned (SASAC ~44 %), SASAC-appointed leadership, Party committee oversight, strategic direction tied to municipal/national objectives.
- **Control typology:** (i) hierarchical state tutelage — **deeper** than Singapore's professional statutory board (0.50) (leadership appointment + Party oversight).
- **Band:** A corporatized authority exists (0.05 excluded), but strategic and leadership decisions lie within the state system → **0.30**.
- **K13 note:** The K10(ii) shareholder relief does not apply — SIPG is not the landlord; moreover, state control is active hierarchical direction, not a passive general-meeting right.
- **Sources:** SIPG (上海国际港务（集团）股份有限公司), 2019 Annual Report (SSE 600018, disclosed 10 Apr 2020; CNINFO, http://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2020-04-10/1207471264.PDF, accessed 2026-11-03): p. 10 "上港集团是上海港公共码头的运营商" (SIPG is the operator of the Port of Shanghai's public terminals; formed Jan 2003 from the Shanghai Port Administration; joint-stock company June 2005; listed on the SSE on 26 Oct 2006 as China's first wholly listed port enterprise); pp. 49–51 top-10 shareholders at 31 Dec 2019 and controlling-shareholder note — Shanghai SASAC 31.36 % direct, plus Tongsheng 4.86 %, Shanghai Chengtou 4.21 %, Shanghai International Group 2.55 % and Shanghai State-owned Assets Operation 0.75 % (all SASAC-controlled) = 43.73 % direct + indirect, "为上港集团的实际控制人（控股股东）" (actual controller / controlling shareholder); Ya Ji Investment 25.15 % + China Merchants Port Development (Shenzhen) 1.62 % (both wholly owned by China Merchants Port Holdings, HK0144, acting in concert); COSCO Shipping 15.00 % (central SOE) → state-linked ≈ 85 %; p. 13 "在上海市委、市政府、市国资委和公司党委、董事会的领导下" (under the leadership of the Shanghai Municipal Party Committee, Municipal Government, Municipal SASAC and the company's Party Committee and Board of Directors) and p. 29 "党建入章" (Party-building written into the articles of association); p. 54 director biographies (Chairman Gu Jinshan concurrently Party Secretary; formerly Deputy Secretary-General of the Shanghai Municipal Government) → state/Party appointment pattern [previously: Wikipedia/SIPG; PitchBook/pestel; Grokipedia — the Grokipedia "corruption investigations" item was not re-sourced; the 2023 investigations fall outside the window].
