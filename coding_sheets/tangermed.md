# ART-41 — Case coding sheet: TANGER MED (#7)
**Coding date:** 2026-07-08 (Batch 2) | **Codebook:** v2.2 | **Status:** locked | **Window:** governance as of end-2019
**Governance pattern:** State-led "private company with public prerogatives" (TMSA) acting as strategic agency and landlord, with plural private global terminal operators.

## Structural summary
- TMSA (Tangier Mediterranean Special Agency) = "private company with public prerogatives", established 2003, under contract with the state.
- 2010 royal decree-law: port-authority powers transferred to Tanger Med Port Authority (a TMSA subsidiary) — a public limited company with its own board of directors.
- Operators: APM Terminals (TM1 + MedPort/TM2), Eurogate/Contship, Marsa Maroc, Tanger Alliance (Marsa Maroc + Eurogate + Contship + Hapag-Lloyd). Plural, world-leading operators.
- Structure: state-led strategic agency BUT plural private concessions at operator level. Royal/ministerial supervisory board (Ministers of the Interior, Economy and Industry + the Hassan II Fund).

## C1 — Regulatory autonomy → **0.50**
- **Subject rule (K9):** Tanger Med Port Authority (TMSA subsidiary) is the landlord and grants concessions to operators → subject = Tanger Med Port Authority.
- **Status:** Public limited company with its own board; authority to grant concessions and develop infrastructure (under the 2010 decree).
- **Control type:** Commercial-company autonomy EXISTS, but the supervisory board is filled with ministers (Interior, Economy, Industry) + royal strategic priority → strong state steering. TMSA carries "public prerogatives".
- **Score: 0.50** — company form + operational/concession autonomy, BUT identity as a state strategic agency + ministerial supervisory board. Gdańsk analogue (corporatized but state-dominant). Higher than Mersin (0.05) because a genuine landlord authority with concession powers exists; lower than Rotterdam because state-strategic steering is intensive.
- Coder's note: the working file asked whether TMSA's public prerogatives and ministerial supervisory board outweigh company autonomy; 0.50 (Gdańsk analogue) confirmed as final (author decision, 2026-07-08; analysed matrix).

## C4 — Compound lock-in → **0.33** (K12)
- **(a) Regulatory rigidity (2005–2019):** 2003 TMSA + 2010 decree + continuous expansion (TM2 in 2019); dynamic legislative/reform capacity → 0.33 (active reform).
- **(b) Financial lock-in (Branch A):** Operator concessions of 30 years (APM MedPort: 2016 contract, opened 2019 → 2049; APM TM1: 2007 → long). ~28–30 years remaining in 2019 → 25–40 band → 0.67. The port is young and expanding, but concession tenors are long → 0.67.
- **(c) Social legitimacy gap (K12, operational):** No operationally effective strike/blockade documented in 2015–2019 (an ITF call exists, but not a cluster of operational disruption) → 0.
- **Composite:** (0.33 + 0.67 + 0)/3 = **0.33**
- Coder's note: C4b = 0.67 on 30-year concession tenors (hence C4 = 0.33) was confirmed (author decision, 2026-07-08; analysed matrix).
