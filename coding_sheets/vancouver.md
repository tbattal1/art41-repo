## Structural summary
- **Landlord:** Vancouver Fraser Port Authority (VFPA) — a Canada Port Authority (Canada Marine Act 1998), "arm's-length federal agency," non-share corporation, self-funding (not tax- or municipally funded), accountable to the federal minister. Created in 2008 by the amalgamation of three port authorities (Vancouver + Fraser River + North Fraser).
- **Operators:** GCT (Deltaport + Vanterm), DP World Canada (Centerm + Fraser Surrey). Multiple independent private operators.
