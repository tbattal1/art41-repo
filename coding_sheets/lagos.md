## Structural summary
- **Landlord + regulator:** Nigerian Ports Authority (NPA) — federal agency (Ports Act 1954), lessor + technical regulator, under the Federal Ministry of Transport. 2006 reform: landlord model, ~25 terminals concessioned to private operators (10–25-year leases).
- **Apapa operators (5 private):** APM Terminals (APMT, flagship container, 25-year concession 2006 → 2031), ENL Consortium (break bulk/general/container), Apapa Bulk Terminals (ABTL), Greenview (GNDL), Lilypond ICT.
