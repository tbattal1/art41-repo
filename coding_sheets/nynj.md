## Structural summary
- **Landlord:** Port Authority of NY & NJ (PANYNJ) — 1921 interstate compact, two-state agency; "typical landlord business model, terminal operations to private companies"; issues bonds, collects leases/fees.
- **Operators:** Maher, APM (Maersk), Port Newark Container Terminal/PNCT (Ports America), Port Liberty Bayonne (GCT). Multiple independent private operators.
