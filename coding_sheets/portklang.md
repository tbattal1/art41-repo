## Structural summary
- **Landlord + regulator:** Port Klang Authority (PKA) — statutory body under the Port Authorities Act 1963, under the (federal) MOT. "Federal regulator + landlord + trade facilitator."
- **Operators (≥2 independent private):** Northport (Malaysia) Bhd (MMC Corporation Berhad) + Westports Malaysia Sdn Bhd (Westports Holdings Berhad, listed; Hutchison-linked).
- **Privatization:** began 1986 (Klang Container Terminal) → 1992 Klang Port Management → 1994 Kelang Multi Terminal (→ Westports) → 2000 Northport (KCT + KPM merger).
