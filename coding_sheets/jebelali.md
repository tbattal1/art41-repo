## Structural summary
- **Ownership chain:** Government of Dubai → Dubai World → Port & Free Zone World FZE (PFZW) → DP World.
- **Status in 2019:** DP World LISTED on Nasdaq Dubai (since 2007; ~20 % float, PFZW majority). Delisting February 2020 → OUTSIDE the window.
- **Functional fusion:** DP World operates Jebel Ali directly (single operator, "common-user" but does not lease to third parties). In the 2005 merger the regulatory/administrative functions were transferred to a separate authority (Dubai Ports & JAFZ Authority / PCFC) — BUT Sultan bin Sulayem chairs both DP World and PCFC → formal separation + fusion at the top.
- **JAFZA:** DP World acquired EZW (the JAFZA operator) in 2015 → port + free-zone integration.
