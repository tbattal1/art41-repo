# ART-41 — Case coding sheet: MERSIN (#8)
**Coding date:** 07.07.2026 (pilot sheet and verification addendum, same day) | **Codebook:** v2 (pilot; cells re-checked against v2.1–v2.6, K1–K16) | **Status:** locked | **Window:** governance as of end-2019 (pre-shock state)
**Coder:** author, with AI coding assistance; author sign-off
**Governance pattern:** fully concessioned port without a port authority — public side = TCDD (grantor/owner, state economic enterprise under the ministry) + Harbour Master's Office (ministry field unit); private side = MIP, single operator under a 36-year operating-rights transfer.

## Structural summary
- No Rotterdam-type "port authority" exists in Mersin. Public side: TCDD (concession grantor/owner, state economic enterprise attached to the ministry) + Harbour Master's Office (ministry field organization). Private side: MIP (Mersin International Port) under a 36-year operating-rights transfer.
- Concession: "36-Year Mersin Port Operating Rights Transfer Agreement" signed 11.05.2007 with the Privatization Administration + TCDD → runs to 2043; consideration USD 755M.
- Operator structure (2019): the whole port under ONE operator (MIP); shareholding PSA 51 % / IFM 39 % / Akfen 10 % (IFM entered in 2017 through Akfen's sale of 40 %).
- Public ownership is central: in the 2021 expansion lawsuit TCDD intervened as "owner of the port".
- A framework Ports Act (Limancılık Kanunu) was drafted repeatedly but never enacted — a documented pattern of blocked framework reform.

## C1 — Regulatory autonomy → **0.05**

**Subject rule applied (K1/K9, Not#5+#12):** in a fully concessioned port without an authority, the C1 subject is the PUBLIC management side (grantor + regulator); the operator's commercial freedom is written to C2, not C1. MIP is a single-operator concessionaire that does not lease or license to third parties → the subject is the public side. (Pilot codebook revision note #5; approved as K1. Rationale: otherwise C1 and C2 would measure the same thing — a multicollinearity-like problem that breaks condition distinctness in fsQCA.)
**Control typology (K10):** type (i) hierarchical tutelage (ministry-attached state economic enterprise, general budget) → reduces autonomy.

## C4 — Compound lock-in → **0.33**

**(a) Regulatory rigidity (window 2005–2019) → 0.67**
One major reform in the 2005–2019 window: the 2007 privatization/operating-rights transfer. BUT the framework Ports Act (Limancılık Kanunu) was drafted repeatedly and never enacted = a documented pattern of failed framework-reform attempts. The v1 rule had not anticipated this combination ("1 reform + a subsequently blocked framework reform").
**Coder's note (codebook revision note #6, resolved as K2):** the proposed v2 rule — a realized reform INSIDE the window gives a base of 0.33; a documented failed FRAMEWORK reform attempt inside the window adds 0.34 → 0.67 — was approved by the author (K2) and entered the codebook C4a table as the row "1 reform + documented failed/blocked subsequent framework reform (Mersin: Ports Act — K2 approved) = 0.67". Mersin C4a = 0.67.

2043 − 2019 = 24 years remaining → [10, 25) band = 0.33. (Second time a case fell on a band boundary; in v2 the band definitions are written as half-open intervals: [10,25), [25,40).)

**(c) Social-legitimacy gap (window 2015–2019) → 0**
No major documented dispute was found in the 2015–2019 window in the web scan. The major events found fall OUTSIDE the window: the Metropolitan Municipality's zoning-plan lawsuit + stay of execution (2021, on Atatürk Park / Millet Bahçesi grounds), and the EIA process for berths 20–21 (2022–23). The author was asked whether any EIA objection to the EMH1 expansion, dockworker action or lawsuit in 2015–2019 was known from local archives/field knowledge; none was recorded, and the cell is 0.
**Coder's note (codebook revision note #7, resolved as Not#7 in v2 — design-level):** the 2021 lawsuit falls before Ş2 (2023Q4–). Single-time-point condition coding (2019) under-measures C4c for Ş2. v2 decision: in the main analysis all conditions are fixed at 2019 (clean design); as a robustness check only the time-varying C4c is re-coded with a 2019–2023 window for the Ş2 run. ## Discussion material
- Financialization: PSA (linked to Singapore's state fund) + IFM (Australian pension fund) → a textbook example of the financialization/consolidation theme, connecting directly to the CFP's "financialization" keyword. The 2019 USD 600M Eurobond (GIHub) is further evidence of financialization.
- Voluntary CSR filling the governance gap (schools, women's shelter, Gates traffic project) in the absence of any structural social-value mandate — a Türkiye-specific finding (Not#8).
- "Legal compliance ≠ coordination density" (C3.4, K11) as the C3 parallel of the de jure/de facto distinction in C5.
- Structural conflict outside the window (non-operational, not in C4c): 2021 zoning-plan lawsuit and stay of execution; 2022–23 EIA process for berths 20–21 — relevant for the Ş2 C4c robustness re-coding (2019–2023 window).
- Contrast with Rotterdam: near mirror image (0.95/0.95/0.95/0.33/0.40 ↔ 0.05/0.70/0.05/0.33/0.05); Mersin is almost a pure "low institutionalization + medium private participation" corner of the configuration space. Excellent for truth-table diversity — the two pilots tested two separate corners.
