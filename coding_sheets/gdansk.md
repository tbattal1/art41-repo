**Governance pattern:** State-Treasury-dominated commercial-company landlord (Port of Gdańsk Authority S.A.) with a single dominant private container operator (DCT Gdańsk).

## Structural summary
- Port of Gdańsk Authority S.A. (ZMPG) was established in 1998 as a commercial company (S.A. = joint-stock company), subject to the Act on Sea Ports and Harbours and the Commercial Companies Code. Shareholders: **the State Treasury (dominant), the Municipality of Gdańsk, and employees.**
- ZMPG acts as landlord and leases to DCT Gdańsk (Baltic Hub), Poland's largest container terminal.
- In May 2019 DCT was acquired by PSA (40 %) + PFR (30 %) + IFM (30 %).
- Polish port policy is centrally set; land reclamation for the terminal required amendments to the Act on Sea Ports (seen in the post-2019 DCT lease case — dependence on national legislation).

## C1 — Regulatory autonomy → **0.50**
- **Subject rule (K9):** ZMPG is the landlord and leases to DCT → subject = ZMPG.
- **Status (2019):** Corporatized commercial company (S.A.) under the Act on Sea Ports and Harbours and the Commercial Companies Code; State Treasury is the dominant shareholder, with the Municipality of Gdańsk and employees as minority shareholders.
- **Control type:** Corporatized (company form favors autonomy) BUT the State Treasury is the dominant shareholder → high central-state tutelage (close to K10 type (i); unlike Rotterdam's municipal shareholder, here the national Treasury dominates). Polish port policy is centralized.
- **Tariff/investment:** The company form confers operational autonomy, but strategic decisions sit within the frame of dominant Treasury ownership and the national ports act. Land reclamation required amendments to the Act on Sea Ports (illustrated by the post-2019 DCT lease case — dependence on the state legislature).
- **Score:** Corporatized but State-Treasury-dominant within a central framework → BELOW Hamburg/Antwerp (0.70, local/regional ownership) but ABOVE Mersin (0.05, no authority). Company form and operational autonomy exist, but national-state tutelage applies → **0.50** (band: "autonomous legal entity exists, but strategic decisions subject to central approval").
